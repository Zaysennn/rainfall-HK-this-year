Daily observations, hourly context.

Rainfall at the Hong Kong Observatory station, in millimetres per calendar day. Verified daily climate records are combined with labelled provisional daily reports and dated current observations. The day in progress is excluded from daily totals. This station is not a city-wide rainfall average; unavailable dates stay empty. The separate hourly model mode contains grid estimates, which never enter measured daily totals or calendar statistics.

The honest edges

Colour uses a shared square-root scale; bar heights and wheel radii use a linear millimetre scale. Two years alone do not establish a climate trend.

Each bar represents one non-overlapping interval in Hong Kong time, 00–01 through 23–24. Only an observation ending exactly on the next hour can fill a slot. Missing records are not zero rain; a daily total is never divided into invented hourly values.

Sources and saved coverage

    Daily climate readings and automatic weather station hourly readings may use different instruments or processing. Their totals need not match. Cloud cover and humidity are daily means; they do not describe every hour. ECMWF IFS hourly model estimates are a separate source, not instrument observations.
